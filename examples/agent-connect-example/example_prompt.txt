Policy
E1 Travel meals: up to $75 per person per day. Alcohol is never reimbursable.
E2 Client meals: up to $120 per attendee, counting employees. Client names must be in the report.

E6 Home office: up to $500 per calendar year for equipment and furniture. Internet and phone bills are not covered.
E7 Receipts and deadlines: an expense of $25 or more without a receipt needs manager approval. Expenses submitted more than 60 days after the date are not reimbursable.
E8 Never reimbursable: fines and tickets, personal subscriptions and entertainment, and anything for personal use.

Apply E7 first, then the rule for the kind of expense. If more than one limit applies, use the lowest.
